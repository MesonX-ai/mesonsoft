# Animated Multiline Text Component: MesonX

This component renders the text **"Meson"** over **"X"** on a new line. The final letter **X** is stylized, emphasized, and animated with a continuous, slow pulse (growing and shrinking). During scaling, it transitions from solid black to a vibrant multi-color gradient and back.

---

## 1. Web Implementation

### HTML
```html
<div class="meson-container">
  <span class="meson-text">Meson</span>
  <span class="meson-x">X</span>
</div>
